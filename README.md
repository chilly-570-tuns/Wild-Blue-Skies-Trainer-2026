## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Wild Blue Skies**. This trainer enhances Wild Blue Skies' arcade flight-combat gameplay with optional tools for surviving missions, managing bombs, improving score runs, and practicing branching routes. It is designed for the game's single-player campaign and supports experimentation without changing the core on-rails flight structure.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to practice missions, explore branching routes, study boss patterns, experiment with bombs and weapons, or reduce the difficulty of the single-player campaign.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Wild Blue Skies uses on-rails flight rather than unrestricted open-world aircraft movement. The aircraft follows a defined mission route while the player moves within the combat space to line up shots, avoid hazards, and respond to incoming attacks. Use the trainer's defensive options to focus on learning movement patterns and stage layouts.

### Tools & Inventory

The game does not use a conventional inventory system. Combat resources are handled during sorties, with bombs serving as a limited-use tool for clearing dangerous groups and surviving demanding encounters. The unlimited-bombs option keeps this resource available throughout a mission.

### World & Environment

Missions travel through varied environments including oceans, desert ridges, and perilous caves. Routes can branch, and secrets or hidden paths reward repeat play. Use route practice and defensive options to explore alternate mission paths and work toward medals without restarting after every mistake.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat centers on shooting waves of enemies, managing targets during fast-moving encounters, using bombs at critical moments, and defeating large bosses. Rapid fire, no cooldown, and one-hit kills accelerate the action, while infinite health provides a safer way to learn attack patterns and timing.
